        Hi! I'm Luis, a maths student in Hong Kong. I love music and I love puzzles, so I built a game that's both: a fight against an alien that only knows the charts, won by the songs you actually love.

        How to play
        
    
          
      
        1. Type a song that answers the request the UFO beams down (a genre, a decade, finish the title, name an artist, and more). That hits The Topline.
          2. 
      
        2. Or type any song by a named Echo walking toward you. That reclaims them on the spot and unleashes their genre's skill on the nameless aliens around them: Power Chord (rock), Bass Drop (electronic), Mic Drop (hip-hop), Blue Note Chain (jazz), Crescendo (classical), Sugar Rush (pop), Smooth Operator (R&B). "Next up" shows who's coming, so you can think ahead.
          3. 
      
        3. Accepted songs join your squad and fight on the beat. Rarer songs hit harder, and songs go stale the longer they stay on stage.
          4. 
      
        4. Don't let Echoes reach your squad. Each one that breaks through makes the crowd forget a little. When the crowd forgets everything, the set is over.
          5. 
      
        5. Genres counter each other: each beats the next one in this ring. The middle squad slot is your Headliner; if it counters the boss's genre, it blocks the boss's attacks.
          6.
